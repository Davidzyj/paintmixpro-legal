お問い合わせ時には、以下の情報を添えてください。

English

For questions about Paint Mix Calculator, please contact the support email address registered on the App Store.

When contacting support, please include the following information:

  • App version you are using
  • iPhone or iPad model
  • iOS version
  • Description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the issue

Calculation results are intended as job-site reference values. Always confirm the final mix with the material manufacturer's specifications and actual site conditions.
